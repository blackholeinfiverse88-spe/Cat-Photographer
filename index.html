Privacy Policy

Cat Photographer is an offline VR game designed for entertainment.

Information We Collect

Cat Photographer does not collect, store, or share personal information. The game does not require user accounts, names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information.

Photos and Game Data

Photos captured within the game are used only for gameplay purposes and are not uploaded to or shared with external servers.

Third-Party Services

The game does not use third-party services to collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Since the game does not collect personal information, no personal data is knowingly collected from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
